Day one

Arrive + Richmond

Wednesday, May 5, 2027
Red-eye arrival
You land early and rooms usually open around 2–3pm. Bags go to the hotel and the day starts in Richmond. Daylight and walking beat a dark room for jet lag.
The Day’s Plan
Early AM
Land + Hotel Drop
Heathrow to the hotel. Hotels hold bags even when the room isn’t ready.
Late AM
Richmond: Ted Lasso Spots
Richmond is about 30 minutes from central London. Paved Court is Ted’s street. The Prince’s Head, a Fuller’s pub at 28 The Green, plays the Crown & Anchor and keeps Ted Lasso memorabilia on the walls. Richmond Green is the backdrop for a lot of the show.
Lunch
Riverside Lunch
Richmond Riverside, then a walk along the Thames.
Afternoon
Kew Gardens or Richmond Theatre
Kew Gardens is next door if you want more green space. Richmond Theatre dates from 1899 and is worth a look from the outside.
Evening
Early Dinner, Early Night
Stay up until it’s dark, then sleep.

Day two

Cotswolds

Thursday, May 6, 2027
Getting there
About two hours each way. After a red-eye, a chauffeured minibus beats driving on the left. A rental car gives you more freedom. Pick one before you go.
The Day’s Plan
Morning
Leave London
Cotswolds minibuses leave from London Euston. A rental car works too.
Late AM
Bibury
Often called one of the most beautiful villages in England. Stone cottages, a river, and not much else, which is the point.
Lunch
Countryside Pub
Pick a pub in whichever village you like most. Skip anything with a coach park.
Afternoon
Bourton-on-the-Water
The “Venice of the Cotswolds”: low stone bridges over a shallow river, small shops, riverside walks.
If time allows
Castle Combe
Thatched-roof village farther south, about 30 minutes from Bath. Only if the day is running well.
Evening
Back to London
About two hours. Easy dinner near the hotel.

Day four

Tower, Bridges, Market, Beer Mile

Saturday, May 8, 2027
Big river day
Everything lines up along the Thames, so the walking stays simple. Saturday is also when most Beer Mile taprooms are open in the daytime.
The Day’s Plan
Morning
Tower of London
The classic London history stop.
Late AM
Tower Bridge + London Bridge
Cross Tower Bridge, then follow the South Bank to London Bridge.
Lunch
Borough Market
Food stalls and a wander. Busy on a Saturday, so go before the noon rush if you can.
Afternoon
Bermondsey Beer Mile
A walkable strip of craft brewery taprooms south of Tower Bridge, and a real local weekend habit. Maltby Street Market (Saturdays and Sundays) is nearby for snacks.
Evening
West End Show or Comedy Club
Optional. It’s a long day on day two of jet lag. Soho Theatre and The Comedy Store are the comedy options.

Day six

Winery Day

Monday, May 10, 2027
Pick one
One winery, reached by train plus a short taxi. No car needed. Tours need booking, so choose first.
The Three Options
Denbies
Surrey · Closest
One of the largest vineyards in England, 265 acres under the North Downs near Box Hill. Ride the estate’s little vineyard train for the outdoor tour, and eat at the restaurant overlooking the vines.
Getting thereWaterloo → Dorking
Best forEasy logistics
Gusbourne
Kent · Best reputation
Often named among the best English sparkling producers. Walk the vineyard slopes, then taste.
Getting thereSt Pancras → Ashford, 40 min, then a 20 min taxi
Best forTop-tier fizz
Nyetimber
West Sussex · Prettiest setting
South Downs views on a medieval estate, with tastings in an old barn. It won world’s best sparkling wine at the 1998 International Wine and Spirits Competition and pours at the Savoy, Ascot and Wimbledon.
Getting thereVictoria → Pulborough, about 80 min, then a short taxi
NoteTours run on select dates
The Day’s Plan
Morning
Pick one
Train Out
Leave London mid-morning for the winery you pick.
Midday
Tour + Tasting
Book the tour once you choose. English wine is mostly sparkling, grown on the same chalk soil as Champagne.
Afternoon
Countryside Lunch
Eat on the way back, or at the estate if it has a restaurant.
Evening
Back in London
Easy night.

Day seven

Stonehenge + Bath

Tuesday, May 11, 2027
Long day, worth it
Stonehenge first, Bath for the afternoon. About 11–12 hours door to door, so wear comfortable shoes and keep dinner easy. A rental car or a driver makes this one work.
The Day’s Plan
Early
Book timed ticket
Stonehenge
About 1.5 hours from London. Entry is timed. Book online the night before or that morning (up to three hours ahead), which is cheaper than the gate. A shuttle runs to the stones, or take the half-hour walk across the meadow. Plan 1–2 hours.
Late AM
Drive to Bath
About an hour from Stonehenge.
Lunch
Lunch in Bath
Sit down somewhere in the center.
Afternoon
Roman Baths
A bathing complex about 2,000 years old that you walk through.
Late afternoon
Georgian Bath
The Royal Crescent and Pulteney Bridge, then a pub stop before the drive back.
Evening
Back to London
About 1.5–2 hours.
